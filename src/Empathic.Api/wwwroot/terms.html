Last updated: 8 September 2026.

This website and its registry are early-stage technology prototypes. By using them, you acknowledge that features may change, data may be reset and development blockchain references may not represent a production public-chain transaction.

No investment offer

EMPATH is currently a working utility-token concept. This website does not offer EMPATH for public sale, does not promise financial returns and does not represent that holding any future token would provide shares, profit participation or ownership in the operating company.

Cultural and intellectual-property rights

Only submit cultural works, metadata, text, images, links or other material that you own, control or are authorised to register. Creating a provenance record does not transfer copyright or prove legal ownership by itself. Rights remain with their lawful owners unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Prototype verification

Hashing and blockchain anchoring can support evidence of integrity and chronology, but they do not independently establish authorship, authenticity, legal title or truth. Human, institutional and documentary verification may still be required.

Proof of Empathy

Proof of Empathy is intended as a framework for verifying eligible participation or evidence defined by a programme. It should not be interpreted as a scientific measurement of a person's emotions, character or moral worth.

The prototype is provided for evaluation and demonstration. It may be modified, suspended or reset as development continues. Production service-level commitments, commercial terms and partner obligations would be documented separately.
