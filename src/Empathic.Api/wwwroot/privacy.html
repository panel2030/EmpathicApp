Privacy notice

How the current website and prototype handle submitted information.

Last updated: 10 September 2026.

The Empathic Movement website is an early-stage platform. The public pages do not use advertising trackers or sell personal information. The website can store details deliberately submitted through the movement-registration form and cultural-registry prototype.

Movement registrations

If you register interest in the movement, the platform can store your name, email address, country, participation category, message and the time of registration. This information is used to record and respond to your request and to support future movement communications where you have agreed to them.

Cultural registry information

The registry prototype can process creator display name, institution, country, roles, cultural-work metadata, external references and content-fingerprint source material. Avoid submitting confidential information, passwords, private keys, sensitive personal data or content you do not have permission to process.

Storage

The current application uses server-side JSON storage for prototype records, including movement registrations. Production deployment should use an appropriately secured production data store, access controls, retention rules, backups and a documented lawful basis for personal-data processing.

Blockchain and hashes

A cryptographic hash can provide tamper-evident evidence without publishing the underlying content. Production deployments should avoid writing personal data directly to an immutable blockchain because deletion and correction rights can conflict with permanent ledgers.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not require analytics cookies. If analytics, CRM, authentication or third-party services are added later, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated before they are enabled.

Your choices

Only submit information you are comfortable providing to the platform. A full production launch should publish a dedicated contact route for access, correction, deletion and other applicable privacy requests.

Production readiness

This notice describes the current prototype and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review. Before collecting community-member information at scale, the founders should complete a data map, retention schedule, processor review, privacy-risk assessment and legal review appropriate to the countries in which the platform operates.
