PROOF OF EMPATHY

Positive participation deserves trusted evidence.

Proof of Empathy is the platform's verification framework for recording that an eligible cultural, educational or community activity happened. It is not a blockchain consensus mechanism and it does not attempt to score a person's emotions.

FAQ

Clear answers, no crypto fog machine.

Is EMPATH an investment token?

No. The current design positions EMPATH as a utility and recognition mechanism. The platform does not promise returns, profit sharing or ownership of the operating company to token holders.

Does Proof of Empathy judge whether someone is empathetic?

No. It verifies eligible participation or evidence defined by a programme. It should not claim to measure a person's inner emotional state.

Why use blockchain at all?

For narrow cases where tamper-evident provenance, credentials or auditability add value. Ordinary application data stays off-chain where that is safer, cheaper and more private.

Can museums, universities or cultural organisations participate?

That is a core intended direction. Partner organisations could register activities, verify participation, issue cultural passports and run branded cultural programmes.
