# Monetization Model

The platform should monetise useful cultural services first; a speculative token is not required.

## Revenue streams

1. **Institutional memberships** — annual subscriptions for cultural institutions, universities and archives.
2. **Creator premium plans** — larger archive quotas, advanced provenance reports and portfolio pages.
3. **Certification fees** — optional fees for enhanced institutional verification or issued certificates.
4. **Licensing marketplace commission** — platform percentage on completed rights/licensing transactions.
5. **Digital editions / collectibles** — platform fee on primary cultural editions where legally appropriate.
6. **Events and cultural tourism** — booking/service fees for workshops, festivals, exhibitions and cultural-pass experiences.
7. **Education and research** — paid courses, research access, institutional datasets and certificate programmes.
8. **Patronage service fee** — small platform fee when supporters fund approved cultural projects.

## Suggested MVP pricing hypothesis for validation

These figures are product hypotheses, not market-validated prices:

- Creator Basic: free
- Creator Pro: €8–15/month
- Institution: €99–299/month
- Enhanced provenance certificate: €5–20 per issue
- Licensing marketplace fee: 5–10%
- Patronage/payment platform fee: 2–5% plus payment-processor charges
- Event marketplace fee: 5–12%

Before charging users, validate willingness-to-pay with artists, universities, municipalities and cultural organisations.
